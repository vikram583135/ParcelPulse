## 1. PURPOSE

The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship related to
**ParcelPulse** — a technology platform for verified advertising placements
on delivery packages. In connection with this opportunity, each party may
disclose certain Confidential Information to the other party.

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed
by either party, including but not limited to:

- **Business Information**: Business plans, strategies, financial information,
  pricing models, revenue projections, customer lists, partnership details,
  and market analysis.

- **Technical Information**: Source code, algorithms, system architecture,
  database schemas, API designs, verification methodologies, GPS/photo/time
  evidence collection pipelines, and all software implementations.

- **Product Information**: Product roadmaps, feature plans, user interface
  designs, mockups, prototypes, and demo presentations.

- **Trade Secrets**: The verified advertising placement system, rider reward
  algorithms, agent distribution logistics, campaign pricing models, and
  all proprietary business methods.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

The Receiving Party agrees to:

a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence.

b) Not disclose any Confidential Information to any third parties without
   the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.

c) Not use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than evaluating
   the potential business relationship described in Section 1.

d) Not copy, reproduce, reverse engineer, decompile, or create derivative
   works from any Confidential Information.

e) Limit access to Confidential Information to those employees, agents, or
   advisors who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality
   obligations at least as restrictive as those contained herein.

f) Immediately notify the Disclosing Party of any unauthorized disclosure
   or use of Confidential Information.

## 4. EXCLUSIONS

Confidential Information does not include information that:

a) Was publicly known at the time of disclosure.

b) Becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party.

c) Was already in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure,
   as evidenced by written records.

d) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference
   to the Confidential Information.

e) Is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided that the
   Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any rights to the
Disclosing Party's intellectual property, including patents, copyrights,
trademarks, or trade secrets. All Confidential Information remains the
exclusive property of the Disclosing Party.

## 6. NO OBLIGATION

This Agreement does not obligate either party to enter into any further
agreements or business relationships.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

a) This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of **2 (two) years**

b) The obligations of confidentiality shall survive termination of this
   Agreement for a period of **3 (three) years**.

c) Upon termination or request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return
   or destroy all Confidential Information and any copies thereof.
